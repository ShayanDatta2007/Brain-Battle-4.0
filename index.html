FAQ

Got
Questions?

Everything you need to know before you step into the arena.

Who can participate in Brain Battle 4.0?+

Open to all UG and PG students from any college or university. No prior quiz experience is required — just your knowledge and the will to win.

Is it individual or team-based?+

Most preliminary rounds are individual. The Buzzer Round allows teams of 2. The Grand Finale is strictly individual. Check each event page for specifics.

What is the registration fee?+

Fee details will be announced shortly on Coding Junction's official channels. Follow us on social media for real-time updates.

What topics should I prepare?+

Cover General Knowledge, Current Affairs (last 6 months), Science & Technology, History & Geography, Sports, and Entertainment. Broad reading is your biggest weapon.

How will I receive my certificate?+

Digital certificates are sent to your registered email within 7 working days after the event. Physical trophies are awarded at the Grand Finale ceremony.

Can I participate from another college?+

Absolutely. Brain Battle 4.0 is an inter-college event and warmly welcomes students from all institutions. The more diverse the field, the more legendary the champion.
